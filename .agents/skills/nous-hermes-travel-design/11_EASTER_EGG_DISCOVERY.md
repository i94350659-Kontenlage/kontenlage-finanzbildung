# NOUS HERMES — EASTER EGG & DISCOVERY ENGINE

## Mission
Create controlled discovery.

## Discovery ladder
LEVEL 1:
looks good without understanding anything.

LEVEL 2:
viewer notices an unusual detail.

LEVEL 3:
viewer realizes it has meaning.

LEVEL 4:
insider understands the reference.

## Easter-egg techniques
- microtype
- altered number
- hidden coordinate
- negative-space symbol
- route hidden inside linework
- badge shape embedded in geometry
- airport code disguised as typography
- date embedded in a grid
- tiny directional marker
- reversed/rotated micro-element
- repeated motif with one intentional exception

## Restraint
Usually one major hidden concept plus a few micro-details is stronger than ten obvious Easter eggs.

## Discovery psychology
The goal is not confusion.
The goal is reward.

The viewer should feel:
"I noticed something."

Not:
"I need to solve a puzzle to understand this shirt."
